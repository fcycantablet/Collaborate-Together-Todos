# Privacy Policy for TodoShare

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **TodoShare** ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. By using TodoShare, you agree to the practices described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use TodoShare, we collect the following information:

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and password (passwords are stored as a one-way hash and are never readable).
- **To-Do Content:** Titles, descriptions, deadlines, priorities, categories, and reminders for the tasks you create.
- **Shared Content:** When you share a to-do with another user, your unique user code is exchanged with that user.
- **Photos:** Images you capture or upload as "proof of completion" for your tasks (up to 10 images per task). These are stored in our database in encoded form, accessible only to you and users you share the task with.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and app version (used solely for diagnosing crashes and improving the App).
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Anonymous device tokens used solely to deliver task reminders and share-related notifications.

We do **NOT** collect:
- Location data
- Contacts
- Browsing history
- Health, financial, or biometric data
- Advertising identifiers

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Save, sync, and display your to-dos across devices
- Allow you to share tasks with friends and family via unique codes
- Deliver reminders, share notifications, and completion alerts
- Diagnose technical issues and improve app stability

We do **NOT**:
- Sell your data to third parties
- Use your data for advertising or marketing
- Track you across other apps or websites

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We only share information in these limited cases:
- **With users you explicitly share to-dos with:** They will see the shared to-do's title, description, deadline, attached images, and your name as the owner.
- **With service providers** that help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting providers, push notification services like Apple APNs and Google FCM). These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality.
- **If required by law:** We will disclose information only if compelled by a valid legal request.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plaintext.
- Authentication uses signed JWT tokens stored securely on your device via Apple Keychain (iOS) or Android Keystore.
- While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. Use a strong, unique password.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- We retain your account and to-do data for as long as your account is active.
- You may delete individual to-dos at any time from within the App.
- To delete your entire account and all associated data, email us at **krmkrmozy@gmail.com** with the subject *"Delete My Account"*. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days.

## 8. Push Notifications

The App may send you push notifications for task reminders and shared task updates. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's system settings.
